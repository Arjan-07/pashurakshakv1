# PashuRakshak UI/UX Refresh

## Risk policy
- Green: 0–30
- Yellow: 31–60
- Red: 61–100
- Risk is hard-capped at 100.
- A potential cluster needs at least 3 related reports and a cluster score of 60+.
- This is surveillance support, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Product changes
Integrated modal forms, toast feedback, dark mode, government-style information hierarchy, role-aware dashboards, risk-zone filtering, print/PDF reports, and removal of automatic browser opening/popups.
